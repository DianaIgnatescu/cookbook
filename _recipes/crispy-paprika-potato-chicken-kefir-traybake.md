Smoky chicken and crackly potatoes on one tray, finished with a cool, garlicky kefir drizzle. Minimal effort, maximum crunch.

**Why it slaps:** the paprika-oiled potatoes catch all the chicken juices while the kefir keeps it fresh.

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 210°C fan. Toss the potatoes with half the oil and a pinch of salt on a big tray, then roast for 10 min.
2. Rub the chicken with the remaining oil, paprika, oregano, salt and pepper.
3. Add the chicken, onion wedges and tomatoes to the tray and shake it all together. Roast for 25 min until golden and cooked through.
4. Stir the garlic and lemon juice into the kefir with a pinch of salt.
5. Serve half the tray with plenty of kefir drizzled over.

**Leftovers:** box the second portion with the kefir kept separate. Reheat at 200°C fan for 12 min (or microwave 2–3 min), then drizzle with the cold kefir.
