Chilli-con-carne vibes with zero heat. Smoky, rich beef and beans piled into fluffy sweet potatoes, with melted cheddar and cool yogurt on top.

**Why it slaps:** the microwave head start means proper jacket potatoes on a weeknight.

## Method
1. Prick the sweet potatoes all over and microwave on high for 8–10 min, turning halfway, until soft. Heat the oven to 220°C fan.
2. Meanwhile, brown the mince in the oil in a big pan over high heat for 5 min, breaking it up. Add the onion and cook for 5 min more.
3. Stir in the garlic, paprika, cumin, oregano and tomato puree for 1 min. Add the tomatoes, beans and a splash of water, season, then simmer for 12–15 min until thick.
4. Put the potatoes in the oven for 8 min so the skins crisp up. Split them open and pile in half the beef with half the cheddar, then return to the oven for 3 min to melt the cheese.
5. Finish with a big spoon of yogurt.

**Leftovers:** box the second potato and the rest of the beef separately. Microwave the potato for 2 min and the beef for 2–3 min, stirring halfway, then add the cheese and yogurt.
