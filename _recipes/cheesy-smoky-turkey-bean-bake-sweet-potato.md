A one-pan, mild and smoky turkey and bean bake with sweet potato, blanketed in melted cheddar.

**Why it slaps:** hearty, cheesy and even better the next day.

## Method
1. Heat the grill to high. Fry the onion and sweet potato in the oil in a large ovenproof pan for 8 min.
2. Add the garlic, paprika and cumin, then the turkey, and break it up for 5 min until no longer pink.
3. Tip in the tomatoes, beans, salt and 100 ml water. Simmer for 15 min until the sweet potato is tender and the sauce is thick.
4. Scatter over the cheddar and grill for 3–4 min until bubbling.
5. Serve half; box the rest.

**Leftovers:** cool and refrigerate within 1 hour. Microwave for 3 min, stirring halfway, until piping hot.
