Everything goes on one tray, the oven does the work, and a cold, tangy feta yogurt brings it all together. Greek-holiday energy on a Monday.

**Why it slaps:** the chicken juices and lemon soak into the potatoes while they crisp up.

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 210°C fan. Toss the potatoes with half the oil and a pinch of salt on a big tray, then roast for 10 min.
2. Meanwhile, rub the chicken with the rest of the oil, the oregano, half the garlic, the lemon zest, salt and pepper.
3. Add the chicken, onion wedges and cherry tomatoes to the tray and give everything a shake. Roast for 25 min until the chicken is golden and cooked through.
4. Crumble the feta into the yogurt with the remaining garlic and a squeeze of lemon, then mash with a fork until it's mostly smooth.
5. Squeeze the rest of the lemon over the tray. Serve half with a big dollop of the feta yogurt.

**Leftovers:** cool and box the second portion with the yogurt kept separate. Reheat at 200°C fan for 12 min (or microwave 2–3 min), then add the cold yogurt on top.
