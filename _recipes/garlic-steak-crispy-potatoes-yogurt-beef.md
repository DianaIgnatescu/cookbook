Steak night without the faff: crispy potatoes, a hot pan and a cold lemony yogurt on the side.

**Why it slaps:** juicy steak, crunchy potatoes and a tangy sauce in 30 minutes.

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C fan. Toss the potatoes with half the oil, oregano and salt on a tray and roast for 25 min, flipping once.
2. Stir the garlic and lemon juice into the yogurt with a pinch of salt.
3. Pat the steak dry and season. Sear in a very hot pan with the rest of the oil for 3 min per side, then rest for 5 min.
4. Wilt the spinach in the hot pan for 30 seconds.
5. Slice the steak and serve with the potatoes, spinach and yogurt.
