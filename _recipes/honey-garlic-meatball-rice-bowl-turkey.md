A Friday fakeaway: glossy, sticky meatballs on fluffy rice with a quick pickled cucumber. Faster than a delivery app.

**Why it slaps:** the honey-soy glaze reduces right in the pan around the meatballs. One pan, no fuss.

## Method
1. Rinse the rice and simmer it in plenty of salted water for 10–12 min, then drain.
2. Mix the turkey with half the garlic, the ginger and half the spring onion. Roll into 10–12 small balls.
3. Fry the meatballs in the sesame oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat for 8–10 min, turning, until browned and cooked through.
4. Whisk the soy, honey, cornflour, the remaining garlic and 50 ml water. Pour it into the pan and bubble for 1–2 min, rolling the meatballs until they're sticky and glazed.
5. Toss the cucumber with the vinegar and a pinch of salt. Serve the meatballs on the rice with the cucumber and the remaining spring onion.
